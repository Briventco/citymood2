What each role can do

Reporter
Writes, edits and publishes their own stories, uploads photos, and can mark their own story as breaking.
Editor
Edits, publishes, schedules and unpublishes any story. Runs the front page and breaking ticker.
Admin
Everything an editor can do, plus staff, categories, ad slots and site settings.
